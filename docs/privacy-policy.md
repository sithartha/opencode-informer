# Privacy Policy — OpenCode Informer

_Last updated: <DATE>_

OpenCode Informer ("the app") is a local companion for the OpenCode developer tool. This
policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Summary

The app **does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data**. There is no account, no
analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

## How the app works

The app communicates only with software you run on your own Mac (the OpenCode CLI and its
companion helper) over your **local network (Wi-Fi)** and **Bluetooth Low Energy**. That
traffic stays on your local network. The app does not connect to the developer's servers or
to any third-party servers.

## Data stored on your device

- A **pairing token** (a random local shared secret) used to authenticate with your Mac on
  the local network, stored in the iOS **Keychain**.
- A **device name** you can edit, and app settings (theme, Lock Screen activity toggle),
  stored locally on the device.
- A short, **in-memory** list of recent agent activity for display; it is not persisted and
  not uploaded.

None of this information leaves your devices.

## Permissions

- **Local Network** — to reach your Mac over Wi-Fi.
- **Bluetooth** — to receive a wake signal from your Mac, including in the background.
- **Notifications** — to alert you when an agent needs attention.

You can change these permissions at any time in iOS **Settings**.

## Children's privacy

The app is a developer tool and is not directed at children. It collects no data from
anyone.

## Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change accordingly. Continued use
of the app means you accept the current policy.
